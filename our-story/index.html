Our Story

Technology these days tends to pull us toward strangers, AI bots, and addictive feeds… often doing more harm than good for our health, and distancing us from the handful of people who really matter.

I’m building Risers to help turn the tide. It’s the place to put your health, and the people who matter most, back in the spotlight.

Here’s how it started.

My name’s Dylan, the builder of Risers.

It goes back to 2016, second year of university in Waterloo, Canada. My girlfriend (now wife) Klaudia and I were “gym buddies”: we got each other’s butts to the gym on the days the other didn’t feel like it. We built amazing consistency…

…until we got internships in different cities. Suddenly, I’d lost my gym buddy.

The idea for an app called “GymBuddies” was born: post a pic once you showed up.

Fitness was the starting point, but the formula of plans + witnesses turned out to be so much bigger. “Huddle” became “Risers”, and my north star has never changed:

What’s the app I’d love to use with Klaudia every day to build consistency together?

Our Ethos

1/ Health is the foundation everything else stands on.

Energy, mood, focus, confidence. Your relationships, your work, your patience on a hard day. All of it sits on top of how you sleep, move, eat, and recover. That’s why Risers is a health app first, not another to-do list. Rise here, and the rest of your life tends to rise with it.

2/ Willpower matters. It’s just rarely enough on its own.

I’ve got plenty of days where willpower alone doesn’t get me out the door. Most of us do. What helps is adding a few more ingredients to the mix: deciding what you’ll do today, and letting someone who cares see it. A plan that’s written down is more likely to happen. A plan that’s witnessed is more likely still. Plans + witnesses isn’t the whole formula for consistency, but it’s one of the most powerful pieces I’ve found. That’s why your daily plans in Risers are shared, not hidden.

3/ Distance shouldn’t dilute your closest bonds.

The idea was born when Klaudia and I were split between cities. But Risers was built and tested from Germany, an ocean away from my family and closest friends back in Canada. I know how easy it is for the people who matter most to drift into “we should catch up soon” territory. Risers gives you a small, daily reason to stay in each other’s lives, rather than a big catch-up every few months.

4/ Technology should bring you closer to real people, not replace them.

No strangers. No follower counts. No algorithm deciding who you see. Your feed in Risers is just the people you chose to let in. I think the best thing a piece of software can do right now is make you need your phone a little less, and the people around you a little more.

Imagining how Risers will help others do the same is what gets me out of bed excited to keep building each day.

We hope you and your inner circle love it too.

Thank you for being part of the Risers community.

— Dylan
